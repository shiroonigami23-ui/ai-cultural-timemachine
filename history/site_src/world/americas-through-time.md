## Achievements before 1492

Mesoamericans domesticated **maize, beans, squash, chillies, tomatoes, cacao and avocados**; Andeans **potatoes, quinoa, llamas and alpacas**. The Maya developed a full **writing system, zero and calendars**; Aztec **chinampas** fed Tenochtitlan (~200,000 people); the Inca built **40,000 km of roads**, terraces and storehouses and kept records on **quipu** knots, all without iron tools, wheels for transport or large draught animals.

## The conquest

Epidemics of smallpox, measles and flu killed perhaps **up to 90%** of the Indigenous population, with conquest and forced labour. The **Columbian Exchange** reshaped diets worldwide. See [The Age of Exploration](/wiki/medieval/age-of-exploration.html).
