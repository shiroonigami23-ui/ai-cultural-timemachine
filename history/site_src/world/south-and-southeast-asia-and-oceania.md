## Indian influence

From about the 1st century CE, traders and priests carried **Sanskrit, Hindu and Buddhist ideas and Brahmi-derived scripts** across the Bay of Bengal. Kingdoms such as Funan, Champa, Srivijaya, the Khmer and Majapahit adopted Indian epics, temple architecture and royal titles. The Ramayana is still performed in Thailand (Ramakien), Cambodia and Bali. Later, Islam spread through trade from Gujarat and Arabia.

## Masters of the ocean

Austronesian and Polynesian navigators settled from Madagascar to Easter Island using outrigger and double-hulled canoes, reading stars, swells, clouds and birds, the greatest maritime expansion before the age of European sail.
