## Technology and knowledge

China gave the world **silk, paper, porcelain, the compass, gunpowder, printing, cast iron, the crossbow and the seismoscope**; Korea **metal movable type** (Jikji, 1377) and the scientific **Hangul** alphabet; Japan refined **woodblock printing, swordsmithing** and later rapid industrialisation.

## How people lived

Most East Asians were farmers growing rice in the south and millet and wheat in the north, organised around families, ancestors and Confucian duties. Scholar-officials chosen by exams ran the Chinese state from the Sui–Tang era to 1905.
