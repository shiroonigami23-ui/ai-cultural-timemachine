## Who Europeans are

Ancient DNA shows most Europeans descend from three groups: **Ice Age hunter-gatherers**, **Neolithic farmers from Anatolia** (from ~7000 BCE) and **Yamnaya steppe herders** (from ~3000 BCE), who probably brought Indo-European languages (Haak et al. 2015).

## Society through time

Classical city-states and Rome built roads, law and aqueducts; medieval feudal society tied peasants to lords and manors; plague raised wages and weakened serfdom; overseas empires and the Atlantic slave trade fed new wealth; industrialisation crowded workers into cities; mass democracy, welfare states and the European Union followed the catastrophes of the 20th century.

Sources: [Haak et al. 2015, Nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14317) · [Wikipedia: History of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Europe)
