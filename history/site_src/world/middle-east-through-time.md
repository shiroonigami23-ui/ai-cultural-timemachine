## Heritage and knowledge

Mesopotamians gave us writing, the wheel, base-60 time, astronomy and law codes; Persians the Royal Road and qanats; Phoenicians the alphabet; Islamic scholars algebra, optics, hospitals and the preservation of Greek and Indian learning. See [The Islamic Golden Age](/wiki/medieval/islamic-golden-age.html).

## Daily life

Farmers in the river valleys grew barley, wheat and dates and paid taxes in grain; nomadic herders of sheep, goats and camels moved between desert and sown land. Cities had bazaars, bathhouses and caravanserais on trade routes linking India, China, Africa and Europe.
