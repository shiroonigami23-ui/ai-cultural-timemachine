## How the steppe shaped history

Horse riding and chariots transformed warfare across Eurasia; steppe migrations probably spread Indo-European languages to Europe and South Asia; nomad empires (Xiongnu, Huns, Turks, Mongols) repeatedly shaped China, Persia, India and Europe. Silk Road cities such as **Samarkand, Bukhara, Merv and Kashgar** were melting pots of Buddhism, Zoroastrianism, Nestorian Christianity, Manichaeism and Islam.
