---
title: Open History Datasets
summary: Free datasets and archives for exploring history and archaeology.
level: Reference
---
| Dataset | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Seshat: Global History Databank](https://seshat-db.com/) | Coded data on hundreds of past societies |
| [Maddison Project Database](https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/) | GDP and population back to 1 CE |
| [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/) | Long-run data on population, health, war, food |
| [Pleiades](https://pleiades.stoa.org/) | Gazetteer of ancient places |
| [Perseus Digital Library](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/) | Greek and Latin texts |
| [GRETIL](http://gretil.sub.uni-goettingen.de/) | Sanskrit and Indic e-texts |
| [Archaeological Survey of India](https://asi.nic.in/) | Indian monuments and excavations |
| [Indian Culture Portal](https://indianculture.gov.in/) | Digitised Indian archives, manuscripts, art |
| [Allen Ancient DNA Resource](https://reich.hms.harvard.edu/allen-ancient-dna-resource-aadr-downloadable-genotypes-present-day-and-ancient-dna-data) | Genotypes of thousands of ancient individuals |
| [Slave Voyages](https://www.slavevoyages.org/) | Records of 36,000 transatlantic slaving voyages |
| [1947 Partition Archive](https://in.1947partitionarchive.org/) | Oral histories of Partition |
| [Vesuvius Challenge](https://scrollprize.org/) | Open scans of the Herculaneum scrolls |
