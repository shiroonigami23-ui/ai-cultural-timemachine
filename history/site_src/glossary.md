# Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **BCE / CE** | Before Common Era / Common Era (same as BC / AD) |
| **Acheulean** | Hand-axe stone-tool tradition of *Homo erectus* and successors |
| **Ancient DNA (aDNA)** | Genetic material recovered from old remains |
| **Axial Age** | 800–200 BCE, when many world philosophies arose |
| **Bronze Age** | Era when bronze tools dominated (~3300–1200 BCE in the Near East) |
| **Chalcolithic** | Copper–stone age |
| **Diwani** | The right to collect revenue, granted to the East India Company in 1765 |
| **Doctrine of Lapse** | Dalhousie's policy of annexing states without a natural heir |
| **Holocene** | The current warm epoch, since 11,700 years ago |
| **Mahajanapada** | One of the 16 great kingdoms of ancient India |
| **Megafauna** | Large animals such as mammoths |
| **Neolithic** | New Stone Age: farming and polished stone tools |
| **Paleolithic** | Old Stone Age |
| **Radiocarbon dating** | Dating organic remains by decay of carbon-14 |
| **Satyagraha** | Gandhi's method of non-violent resistance |
| **Stratigraphy** | Reading layers of soil to date finds |
| **Zamindar** | Landholder collecting revenue |
