Human evolution is not a ladder but a **bushy tree**: many species branched off, overlapped and went extinct. For most of the last two million years **several kinds of humans lived at the same time**. Browse them all in the [Human species catalog](/wiki/hominins/index.html).

## The main branches

| Group | When | Key species | What changed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earliest hominins | 7–4.4 million years ago | *Sahelanthropus*, *Orrorin*, *Ardipithecus* | Beginnings of upright walking |
| Australopithecines | 4.2–2 million years ago | *A. anamensis*, *A. afarensis* (Lucy), *A. africanus*, *A. sediba* | Habitual bipedalism, small brains |
| Robust hominins | 2.7–1 million years ago | *Paranthropus aethiopicus*, *boisei*, *robustus* | Huge jaws for tough plant food; a dead-end branch |
| Early *Homo* | 2.4–1.4 million years ago | *H. habilis*, *H. rudolfensis* | Stone tools, larger brains |
| *Homo erectus* group | 1.9 million – 110,000 years ago | *H. ergaster*, *H. erectus*, Dmanisi people | Human body, fire, left Africa |
| Archaic humans | 700,000 – 40,000 years ago | *H. heidelbergensis*, Neanderthals, Denisovans, *H. naledi*, *H. floresiensis*, *H. luzonensis* | Large brains (Neanderthals), island dwarfs |
| Modern humans | 300,000 years ago – today | *Homo sapiens* | Symbolic culture, global spread |

## Who met whom

:::collapse !Our extended family
- **Neanderthals** lived in Europe and western Asia from ~400,000 to ~40,000 years ago. They made spears and glue, controlled fire, buried their dead and decorated themselves. When *Homo sapiens* spread out of Africa, the two met; most people outside Africa carry about **1–2% Neanderthal DNA**.
- **Denisovans** are known mainly from a finger bone and teeth from **Denisova Cave** (Siberia), a jaw from Tibet and, since 2025, the **Harbin skull** ("Dragon Man") identified as Denisovan from proteins and DNA. Papuans and Aboriginal Australians carry up to ~4–6% Denisovan DNA; Tibetans owe their high-altitude gene *EPAS1* to them.
- **Homo floresiensis** (1 m tall) lived on Flores until ~50,000 years ago, and **Homo luzonensis** on Luzon.
- In India, the **Narmada skull** from Hathnora (Madhya Pradesh) is an archaic human whose species is still debated.
:::

## Why only one of us is left

Explanations include our larger social networks, more flexible technology and long-distance trade, faster population growth, climate swings that fragmented small archaic populations, and interbreeding that absorbed them. In a sense Neanderthals and Denisovans did not entirely vanish: they live on in our genes.
