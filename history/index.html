About this encyclopedia#

The Cultural Time Machine is a long-form, sourced encyclopedia of human history and culture. It is part of the AI Cultural Time Machine project.

  • Every era, from Pangaea and human origins through the Ice Age to the Information Age. Each covers how people lived, what they ate, and the species of the time.
  • 86 civilisations and empires, with capitals, achievements, daily life and food.
  • 101 turning points: ice ages, eruptions and droughts; plagues and pandemics; the Roman conquests; the East India Company and the 1857 uprising; the world wars, Partition and more.
  • People, sites, festivals, peoples and tribes, religions, scripts, inventions and extinct species.
  • Long articles on India’s history, daily life, food and the turning points of world history.
  • A live archaeology tracker, animated statistics, and live photos, full Wikipedia articles and the latest research papers on every catalog page.
