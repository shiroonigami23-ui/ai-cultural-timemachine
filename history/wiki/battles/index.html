⚔ Battles, Sieges & Betrayals

The battles and sieges that changed history, told without the glory filter: Thermopylae and Ephialtes's betrayal, Cannae, the Teutoburg ambush, Karbala, Hattin, Agincourt and the killing of prisoners, the Black Dinner, the fall of Constantinople, Tarain, Chittorgarh and its jauhar, Talikota, Plassey and Mir Jafar, Panipat, Saraighat, Seringapatam and Mir Sadiq, Cawnpore and Bibighar, Cajamarca, Glencoe, Wounded Knee, the Somme, Gallipoli, Nanjing, Stalingrad, Dresden, My Lai, Bangladesh 1971 and Srebrenica. Each covers the sides, commanders, forces and casualties; how it was fought; the traitors and backstabbing; the atrocities and war crimes; and how it reshaped history.

Ancient & classical battles

Battle of Thermopylae

A small Greek army held the narrow coastal pass of Thermopylae for three days against Xerxes' invasion, until a local man showed the Persians a mountain path around them.

Ancient & classical battles

Battle of Cannae

Hannibal annihilated the largest army Rome had ever fielded with a double envelopment, still studied in military academies.

Ancient & classical battles

Battle of the Teutoburg Forest

Three Roman legions were ambushed and destroyed over three days in forest and marsh — Rome's worst defeat under Augustus.

Ancient & classical battles

Siege of Jerusalem (70 CE)

Rome crushed the Jewish revolt by besieging Jerusalem and destroying the Second Temple.

Ancient & classical battles

Siege of Masada

The last rebel stronghold on a desert mesa fell after the Romans built a gigantic siege ramp.

Ancient & classical battles

Battle of Kalinga

Ashoka's conquest of Kalinga was so bloody that it changed his life and the history of Buddhism.

Medieval battles & sieges

Battle of Karbala

Husayn, grandson of the Prophet Muhammad, refused to pledge allegiance to Yazid and was killed with his small band — the defining tragedy of Shia Islam.

Medieval battles & sieges

Battle of Hattin

Saladin destroyed the crusader army after luring it into a waterless march in midsummer, leading to the fall of Jerusalem.

Medieval battles & sieges

Battle of Agincourt

A sick, starving English army defeated a much larger French force in a muddy field — immortalised by Shakespeare's Henry V.

Medieval battles & sieges

The Black Dinner

The young Earl of Douglas was invited to dine with the 10-year-old King James II and then murdered — a famous betrayal of hospitality that inspired the 'Red Wedding' in Game of Thrones.

Medieval battles & sieges

Fall of Constantinople (siege)

Ottoman cannon broke the thousand-year-old Theodosian Walls.

Indian subcontinent

Second Battle of Tarain (battle)

A year after losing at Tarain, Muhammad of Ghor returned and defeated Prithviraj, opening north India to the Delhi Sultanate.

Indian subcontinent

Siege of Chittorgarh (1568)

Akbar took Chittor, Mewar's great hill fort, after a four-month siege, the third time the fort fell.

Indian subcontinent

Battle of Plassey (battle)

A battle won mainly by bribery opened the way to British rule in India.

Indian subcontinent

Siege of Cawnpore and Bibighar

After a three-week siege the British surrendered on a promise of safe passage, which ended in massacre on both sides.

Early modern & colonial

Ambush at Cajamarca

Pizarro captured Atahualpa in a surprise attack disguised as a meeting.

Early modern & colonial

Glencoe Massacre

Soldiers who had accepted MacDonald hospitality for two weeks rose before dawn and killed their hosts.

Early modern & colonial

Wounded Knee Massacre

US soldiers killed Lakota men, women and children while disarming their band.

World wars & modern conflicts

Battle of the Somme

One of the bloodiest battles in history, fought for a few kilometres of mud.

World wars & modern conflicts

Gallipoli campaign

An Allied attempt to seize the Dardanelles and knock the Ottomans out of the war failed.

World wars & modern conflicts

Nanjing Massacre

After capturing China's capital, Japanese troops committed mass murder and rape over six weeks.

World wars & modern conflicts

Battle of Stalingrad (battle)

The turning point of the Eastern Front.

World wars & modern conflicts

Bombing of Dresden

Allied firebombing destroyed the historic city centre.

World wars & modern conflicts

My Lai Massacre

US soldiers murdered hundreds of unarmed villagers, mostly women, children and elderly.

World wars & modern conflicts

Operation Searchlight and the 1971 genocide

Pakistan's army launched a crackdown on Bengali nationalists that became genocide.

World wars & modern conflicts

Srebrenica massacre

The worst massacre in Europe since WWII, in a UN 'safe area'.

Indian subcontinent

Battle of Talikota (battle)

An alliance of Deccan sultans destroyed Vijayanagara's army, and its capital Hampi was sacked for months.

Third Battle of Panipat (battle)

One of the largest battles of the 18th century crushed Maratha expansion in the north.

Battle of Saraighat

A naval battle on the Brahmaputra in which the Ahoms stopped the Mughals from conquering Assam.

Indian subcontinent

Siege of Seringapatam (1799)

The British stormed Tipu Sultan's island capital and killed him at the walls.
