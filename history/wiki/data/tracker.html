1 · Herculaneum scrolls
**Status:** On **25 June 2026** in Naples, the **Vesuvius Challenge** announced that scroll **PHerc. 1667** had been virtually unwrapped and read **from beginning to end**, the first Herculaneum papyrus read in full without being opened. Another scroll was identified as Philodemus, *On Gods*, Book 8. The work used X-ray scans (including ESRF's BM18 beamline) and machine learning, building on Brent Seales's virtual-unwrapping research at the University of Kentucky. The challenge has awarded about **$1.8 million** in prizes since 2023. **Next:** More than **600** Herculaneum scrolls remain unopened. Sources: [Vesuvius Challenge: "We read an entire scroll"](https://scrollprize.org/firstscroll) · [ESRF](https://www.esrf.fr/home/news/general/content-news/general/the-day-the-herculaneum-scrolls-began-speaking-again.html) · [Reuters via Express Tribune](https://tribune.com.pk/story/2615079/researchers-use-ai-to-unlock-the-complete-text-of-burnt-herculaneum-scroll-for-the-first-time) · [Pompeii and Vesuvius](/wiki/events/eruption-of-vesuvius.html)
2 · Keeladi (Tamil Nadu)
**Status:** The Tamil Nadu State Department of Archaeology began the **11th excavation phase** on **18 March 2026**. Excavators exposed continuous brick structures in all nine quadrants, a terracotta pipeline and closed drain, and more than **1,300 artefacts**, including beads, spindle whorls, iron knives and swords, ivory dice and gold ornaments. An **iron-working furnace** was reported in July 2026. Madurai Kamaraj University's ancient-DNA lab has extracted about **75,000 genetic markers** from the Konthagai burial site; results so far show affinity with present-day South Indians and Sri Lankan Tamils, with papers expected by December 2026. Sources: [South First](https://thesouthfirst.com/tamilnadu/keeladi-excavation-unearths-rare-furnace-reinforcing-evidence-of-early-tamil-iron-production/) · [Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/amp/story/india/tamil-nadu/75000-markers-decoded-genetic-roots-of-ancient-tamils-unveiled-4099243) · [Sangam-era Tamilakam](/wiki/civilizations/sangam-era-tamilakam.html)
3 · Indus script
**Status:** About 400 signs on 4,000+ objects; no bilingual text has been found and inscriptions average only about five signs, so the script remains undeciphered. Sources: [Indus script](/wiki/beliefs/indus-script.html) · [Indus Valley Civilisation](/wiki/ancient/indus-valley-civilisation.html)
4 · Ancient DNA of South Asia
**Status:** The Rakhigarhi genome (Shinde et al. 2019) and the 2019 South and Central Asia study (Narasimhan et al.) reshaped understanding of Indian ancestry; Tamil Nadu labs are now analysing Sangam-era burials. Sources: [Research papers](/wiki/papers/index.html)
