📜 Manuscripts & Texts

The manuscripts, tablets, scrolls, inscriptions and codices through which we know the past: the Pyramid Texts and Gilgamesh, the Rigveda and Ashoka's edicts, oracle bones and the Diamond Sutra, the Dead Sea Scrolls, Codex Sinaiticus, the Quran's oldest folios, the Timbuktu manuscripts and the Maya and Aztec codices — with language, script, material, where each is kept today, what it says and why it matters.
