Religions & philosophies

Animism and shamanism

Probably humanity's oldest religious outlook: the belief that animals, plants, rivers and mountains have spirits, with shamans entering trance to heal and to speak with spirits. Evidence includes Upper Palaeolithic cave art and burials with grave goods. It survives in many indigenous religions, from Siberia to the Amazon and India's Sarna faith.

Religions & philosophies

Mesopotamian religion

Each city had a patron god — Enlil at Nippur, Inanna at Uruk, Marduk at Babylon — housed in a ziggurat temple. Humans were believed to be created to serve the gods, and priests read omens in livers and stars, laying foundations for astronomy.

Religions & philosophies

Vedic religion

Worship centred on fire sacrifices (yajna) to gods such as Agni, Indra and Varuna, led by priests chanting Vedic hymns. Later the Upanishads turned inward to Brahman and Atman, and Vedic ritual evolved into classical Hinduism.

Religions & philosophies

Ajivika

A rival of Buddhism and Jainism that taught strict fate (niyati): all events are predetermined. Ashoka's grandson gave them the Barabar caves.

Religions & philosophies

Charvaka (Lokayata)

Ancient Indian materialism: only perception is valid knowledge, there is no afterlife or soul, and pleasure is the aim of life.

Religions & philosophies

Manichaeism

Mani taught a dualism of light and darkness, drawing on Zoroastrian, Christian and Buddhist ideas; it spread from Rome to China before persecution destroyed it.

Religions & philosophies

Mahayana Buddhism

The 'Great Vehicle' emphasised the bodhisattva ideal of saving all beings and spread to China, Korea, Japan and Tibet.

Religions & philosophies

Shia and Sunni split

Disagreement over who should lead Muslims after Muhammad — elected caliphs or Ali's family — divided Sunni and Shia Islam; Husain's death at Karbala (680) deepened it.

Religions & philosophies

Protestantism

Luther's Ninety-five Theses challenged indulgences and papal authority, launching the Reformation and new churches.

Religions & philosophies

Bahá'í Faith

Teaches the unity of God, religions and humanity.

Religions & philosophies

Brahmo Samaj and Arya Samaj

19th-century Hindu reform movements against idol worship, caste and child marriage.

Religions & philosophies
