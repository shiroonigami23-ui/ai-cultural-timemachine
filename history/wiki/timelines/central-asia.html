Timeline: Central Asia

Every catalogued civilisation, dynasty, event, person, monument, invention and human species in Central Asia, from the earliest evidence to today (14 entries).

📅 Reviewed 2026-11-15⏱ 1 min read🔗 0 sources🎓 Timeline

Every catalogued civilisation, dynasty, event, person, monument, invention and human species in Central Asia, from the earliest evidence to today (14 entries).

Deep prehistory (before 10,000 BCE) (4)
1.9 Myr ago: 📅 Migration: Homo erectus leaves Africa — First humans in Eurasia
1.9 Myr ago: 🦴 Human species appears: Homo georgicus (Dmanisi) — Georgia
300,000 yr ago: 🦴 Human species appears: Denisovans — Siberia; Tibet; Southeast Asia
23,000 yr ago: 📅 Domestication: Dog domestication — First domestic animal
Bronze Age (3000–1200 BCE) (1)
2200 BCE: 📅 Domestication: Horse domestication — Mobility, warfare, chariots
Classical age (500 BCE–500 CE) (4)
138 BCE: 📅 Trade: Silk Road opened (Zhang Qian) — Eurasian trade network
30 CE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Kushan Empire — c. 30–375 CE
30 CE: 👑 Dynasty: Kushan dynasty — The Yuezhi nomads, pushed from China's frontier, conquered Bactria; Kujula Kadphises united them in the 1st century CE.
440 CE: 👑 Dynasty: Hephthalites (White Huns) — Central Asian nomads who defeated the Sasanians in 484 and, under Toramana and Mihirakula, raided Gupta India.
Early medieval (500–1000) (2)
552 CE: 👑 Dynasty: Göktürk Khaganate — Bumin Qaghan revolted against the Rouran (552). The Orkhon inscriptions are the earliest written Turkic.
751 CE: 📅 Technology transfer: Tang–Abbasid paper transfer — Paper reaches the Islamic world and Europe
Late medieval (1000–1500) (2)
1336: 👤 Born: Timur — Timurid Empire
1370: 👑 Dynasty: Timurid Empire — Timur conquered from Delhi to Anatolia and made Samarkand dazzling. His grandson Ulugh Beg built an observatory.
Contemporary (1945–today) (1)
1979: 📅 Invasion: Soviet invasion of Afghanistan — Cold War proxy war
