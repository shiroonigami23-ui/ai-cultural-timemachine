The Branching Timeline of Humanity

Civilisations and dynasties of every region side by side from 3500 BCE to today, with region-by-region timelines of every event.

📅 Reviewed 2026-11-15⏱ 92 min read🔗 0 sources🎓 Timeline

Human history did not move in step everywhere. Farming, cities, iron, writing and industry arrived at different times in different regions, so this timeline branches by region. Each bar is a civilisation (amber) or dynasty (blue); hover for dates and click to open it. Below are the region timelines, each with every catalogued civilisation, dynasty, event, person, monument, invention and human species, sorted by date.

Region timelines: Africa · Middle East · Europe · South Asia · Central Asia · East Asia · Southeast Asia · Americas · Oceania · Global

Theme timelines: Religions · Languages and writing · Technology · Manuscripts · Rulers and dynasties · Wars, plagues and disasters · Hidden history and conspiracies · Engineering · Invasions and conquests · Battles, betrayals and atrocities · Myths and legendary creatures

Not everyone evolved at the same rate: Mesopotamia and Egypt had cities and writing by 3200 BCE, while much of Europe, the Americas and Africa south of the Sahara were still farming villages or hunter-gatherer lands; Australia’s peoples kept a hunter-gatherer way of life for 65,000 years; and Polynesians reached New Zealand only around 1300 CE. Each branch below follows its own path.
