Timeline: Americas

Every catalogued civilisation, dynasty, event, person, monument, invention and human species in Americas, from the earliest evidence to today (103 entries).

📅 Reviewed 2026-11-15⏱ 6 min read🔗 0 sources🎓 Timeline

Every catalogued civilisation, dynasty, event, person, monument, invention and human species in Americas, from the earliest evidence to today (103 entries).

Deep prehistory (before 10,000 BCE) (3)
21,000 yr ago: 📅 Migration: White Sands footprints — Earliest firm evidence of people in the Americas
16,000 yr ago: 📅 Migration: Peopling of the Americas — Humans reach the last inhabited continents
11,000 yr ago: 📅 Culture: Clovis culture — Big-game hunting of mammoths
Neolithic and first farmers (10,000–3000 BCE) (2)
7000 BCE: 📅 Agriculture: Maize domestication — Staple of the Americas
3500 BCE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Norte Chico (Caral) — c. 3500–1800 BCE
Bronze Age (3000–1200 BCE) (2)
2000 BCE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Maya civilisation — c. 2000 BCE–1697 CE (Classic 250–900)
1600 BCE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Olmec — c. 1600–350 BCE
Classical age (500 BCE–500 CE) (5)
400 BCE: 🗿 Built / founded: Tikal — Maya
100 BCE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Teotihuacan — c. 100 BCE–550 CE
100 BCE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Ancestral Puebloans — c. 100–1600 CE
100 BCE: 👑 Dynasty: Maya dynasties of Tikal — Villages from about 2000 BCE became cities such as El Mirador in the Preclassic period.
100 BCE: 🗿 Built / founded: Teotihuacan — Teotihuacanos
Early medieval (500–1000) (2)
600 CE: 🗿 Built / founded: Chichén Itzá — Maya
800 CE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Mississippian culture — c. 800–1600 CE
Late medieval (1000–1500) (15)
1000: 📅 Exploration: Norse reach North America — First European landing in the Americas
1200: 📜 Manuscript: Dresden Codex — 11th–12th century
1250: 🗿 Built / founded: Moai of Easter Island — Rapa Nui
1325: 📅 City founding: Founding of Tenochtitlan — Capital of the Aztec empire
1376: 👑 Dynasty: Aztec rulers (Tlatoani) — The Mexica founded Tenochtitlan in 1325 and the Triple Alliance formed in 1428.
1418: 👤 Born: Pachacuti — Built the Inca Empire
1428: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Aztec Empire — 1428–1521
1438: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Inca Empire — 1438–1533
1438: 👑 Dynasty: Inca Sapa Inca line — Pachacuti (r. 1438–1471) turned the Cusco kingdom into an empire.
1450: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Haudenosaunee Confederacy — c. 1450 (or earlier) – today
1450: 🗿 Built / founded: Machu Picchu — Pachacuti
1450: 🏗 Built: Machu Picchu — Peru
1450: 🏗 Built: Q'eswachaka rope bridge — Apurímac River, Peru
1466: 👤 Born: Moctezuma II — Met Cortés
1492: 📅 Exploration: Columbus reaches the Americas — Columbian Exchange begins
Early modern (1500–1800) (15)
1500: 🐉 Myth: Thunderbird — North America
1502: 👤 Born: Atahualpa — Captured by Pizarro
1521: 📅 Conquest: Fall of Tenochtitlan — End of the Aztec Empire
1532: 📅 Conquest: Spanish conquest of the Inca — End of the Inca Empire
1532: ⚔ Battle: Ambush at Cajamarca — Spanish conquest of the Inca
1541: 📜 Manuscript: Codex Mendoza — c. 1541
1550: 📜 Manuscript: Popol Vuh — c. 1550s; Ximénez copy c. 1701
1577: 📜 Manuscript: Florentine Codex — 1545–1577
1600: 🐉 Myth: Wendigo — Northern North America
1607: 📅 Colonialism: Jamestown founded — English America
1741: 👤 Born: Benedict Arnold — Plotting to surrender West Point to the British
1763: 🔓 Hidden history: Smallpox blankets — Partly true
1775: 📅 Revolution: American Revolution — Independence of the USA
1776: 🏛 Civilisation begins: United States — 1776–today
1783: 👤 Born: Simón Bolívar — Independence of six nations
Industrial and imperial age (1800–1945) (32)
1810: 📅 Independence: Latin American independence — New republics
1846: ⚙ Invention: Anaesthesia — USA
1847: 👤 Born: Alexander Graham Bell — The telephone
1861: 📅 War: American Civil War — Ended slavery; ~620,000+ dead
1863: 👤 Born: Henry Ford — Mass-produced cars
1867: 👤 Born: Wright brothers — First controlled powered aeroplane
1871: 🔓 Hidden history: Meucci invented the telephone — Partly true
1873: 💞 Society: Comstock laws — 1873
1876: 📅 Invention: First telephone call — Birth of telecommunications
1876: ⚙ Invention: Telephone — USA
1879: 👤 Born: Margaret Sanger — Founding the birth-control movement
1882: 📅 Invention: Edison's light and Pearl Street station — Start of electric utilities
1882: 👤 Born: Franklin D. Roosevelt — New Deal, WWII
1883: 🏗 Built: Brooklyn Bridge — New York, USA
1884: 🔓 Hidden history: Edison stole Tesla's inventions — Mostly myth
1888: ⚙ Invention: Alternating current power grid — USA
1890: ⚔ Battle: Wounded Knee Massacre — American Indian Wars
1894: 👤 Born: Alfred Kinsey — The Kinsey Reports
1895: 📅 Invention: Niagara Falls power plant — Victory of alternating current
1903: 📅 Technology: Wright brothers' flight — Powered flight
1903: ⚙ Invention: Aeroplane — USA
1904: 👤 Born: J. Robert Oppenheimer — Director of Los Alamos; 'father of the atomic bomb'
1917: 🔓 Hidden history: Radium Girls — Documented fact
1926: 👤 Born: Fidel Castro — Cuban Revolution
1928: 👤 Born: Che Guevara — Cuban Revolution
1929: 👤 Born: Martin Luther King Jr. — 'I Have a Dream'
1932: 🔓 Hidden history: Tuskegee syphilis study — Documented fact
1937: 🏗 Built: Golden Gate Bridge — San Francisco, USA
1940: 🏗 Built: Tacoma Narrows Bridge collapse — Washington, USA
1942: 📅 Science and war: Manhattan Project begins — Race for the atomic bomb
1942: ⚙ Invention: Nuclear energy — USA
1943: 🔓 Hidden history: Tesla's seized papers — Documented fact (myth exaggerated)
Contemporary (1945–today) (27)
1945: 📅 Science and war: Trinity nuclear test — The first nuclear explosion
1945: ⚙ Invention: Atomic bomb — Los Alamos, USA
1947: ⚙ Invention: Transistor — USA
1947: 🔓 Hidden history: Area 51 and Roswell — Partly true
1948: 💞 Society: Kinsey Reports — 1948 and 1953
1950: 🔓 Hidden history: Lavender scare — Documented fact
1955: 👤 Born: Steve Jobs — Apple, the iPhone
1955: ⚙ Invention: Polio vaccine — USA
1956: 🔓 Hidden history: COINTELPRO — Documented fact
1958: ⚙ Invention: Microchip — USA
1958: 🐉 Myth: Bigfoot (Sasquatch) — Pacific Northwest, USA and Canada
1960: ⚙ Invention: Contraceptive pill — USA
1960: 💞 Society: The birth-control pill — Approved in the USA 1960
1962: 📅 Cold War: Cuban Missile Crisis — Nuclear brink
1962: 🔓 Hidden history: Operation Northwoods — Documented fact (proposed, rejected)
1963: 🔓 Hidden history: JFK assassination — Disputed
1966: 🏗 Built: Cheyenne Mountain Complex — Colorado, USA
1969: 📅 Exploration: Moon landing — First humans on the Moon
1969: 📅 Invention: ARPANET's first message — Birth of the internet
1969: ⚙ Invention: Internet — USA, CERN
1969: 💞 Society: Stonewall riots — 28 June 1969
1972: 🔓 Hidden history: Watergate — Documented fact
1995: 🐉 Myth: Chupacabra — Puerto Rico and Latin America
2001: 📅 Terrorism: September 11 attacks — ~3,000 killed; wars in Afghanistan and Iraq
2007: 📅 Invention: Launch of the iPhone — Smartphone revolution
2007: ⚙ Invention: Smartphone — USA
2018: ⚙ Invention: Large language models — USA and worldwide
