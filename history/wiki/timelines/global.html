Deep prehistory (before 10,000 BCE) (7)
200 Myr ago: 📅 Continental drift: Breakup of Pangaea — Shaped today's continents and oceans
66 Myr ago: 📅 Mass extinction: Chicxulub asteroid impact — ~75% of species lost, incl. non-avian dinosaurs
2.6 Myr ago: 📅 Ice age: Onset of the Quaternary ice ages — Repeated glaciations shaped human evolution
60,000 yr ago: 📅 Migration: Out of Africa migration — All non-African populations descend from it
50,000 yr ago: ⚙ Invention: Sewing needle — Siberia (Denisova Cave)
20,000 yr ago: 📅 Ice age: Last Glacial Maximum — Sea level ~120 m lower; land bridges
10,900 yr ago: 📅 Cold snap: Younger Dryas — Abrupt return to near-glacial cold
