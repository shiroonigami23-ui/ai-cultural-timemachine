400,000 yr ago: 🦴 Human species appears: Neanderthals — Europe; Western and Central Asia
300,000 yr ago: 📅 Technology: Schöningen spears — Big-game hunting
40,000 yr ago: 📅 Extinction: Neanderthal extinction — Homo sapiens alone in Europe
40,000 yr ago: ⚙ Invention: Musical instruments (flutes) — Germany
36,000 yr ago: 📅 Culture: Chauvet Cave paintings — Masterful Ice Age art

5000 BCE: 📅 Technology: Copper smelting — Start of metallurgy
3500 BCE: ⚙ Invention: Wheel — Mesopotamia / Eastern Europe
3100 BCE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Minoan civilisation — c. 3100–1100 BCE

3000 BCE: 🗿 Built / founded: Stonehenge — Neolithic Britons

1750 BCE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Mycenaean Greece — c. 1750–1050 BCE
1600 BCE: 📅 Natural disaster: Thera eruption — Minoan decline

508 BCE: 📅 Politics: Athenian democracy — Rule by citizens

476 CE: 📅 Collapse: Fall of the Western Roman Empire — Start of the Middle Ages in the West

732 CE: 📅 War: Battle of Tours — Halted Umayyad expansion into Francia
751 CE: 👑 Dynasty: Carolingian dynasty — Mayors of the palace replaced the Merovingians.

793 CE: 📅 War: Viking raid on Lindisfarne — Viking Age begins
800 CE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Holy Roman Empire — 800–1806
800 CE: 📅 Politics: Charlemagne crowned emperor — Holy Roman Empire

886 CE: 👑 Dynasty: House of Wessex and Anglo-Saxon England — Resisted the Vikings.
960 CE: 👑 Dynasty: Piast and Jagiellonian Poland — Christianisation and union with Lithuania.

1066: 👑 Dynasty: Normans and Plantagenets — Norman invasion.
1066: 📅 Conquest: Battle of Hastings — Norman conquest
1204: 📅 War: Fourth Crusade sacks Constantinople — Byzantine collapse
1215: 📅 Law: Magna Carta — Limited royal power
1259: 👑 Dynasty: Palaiologos dynasty — Recaptured Constantinople from the Latins.
1270: ⚙ Invention: Mechanical clock — Europe
1273: 👑 Dynasty: Habsburg dynasty — Clever marriages ('Let others wage war; you, happy Austria, marry').
1286: ⚙ Invention: Spectacles — Italy

1452: 👤 Born: Leonardo da Vinci — Mona Lisa, notebooks
1473: 👤 Born: Nicolaus Copernicus — Sun-centred universe
1485: 👑 Dynasty: Tudor dynasty — Won Bosworth.
1492: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Spanish Empire — 1492–1976
1492: 📅 War: Fall of Granada and Reconquista — End of Muslim rule in Iberia

1643: 👤 Born: Isaac Newton — Gravity, calculus, optics

1721: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Russian Empire — 1721–1917
1769: 👤 Born: Napoleon Bonaparte — Napoleonic Code, conquests
1776: 📅 Technology: Watt's steam engine — Industrial power
1789: 📅 Revolution: French Revolution — End of absolute monarchy; 'Liberty, equality, fraternity'
1796: ⚙ Invention: Vaccination — England
