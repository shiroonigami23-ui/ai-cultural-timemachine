105 CE: ⚙ Invention: Paper — China
184 CE: 📅 Uprising: Yellow Turban rebellion — Han collapse
250 CE: 👑 Dynasty: Yamato and imperial Japan — The Yamato kingdom unified Japan in the 3rd–6th centuries; the emperor line is the world's oldest hereditary monarchy.
Early medieval (500–1000) (14)
581 CE: 👑 Dynasty: Sui dynasty — Yang Jian (Emperor Wen) reunified China in 589 and restored examinations.
618 CE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Tang dynasty — 618–907
618 CE: 👑 Dynasty: Tang dynasty — After the short Sui dynasty reunified China (581) but exhausted it with huge canal projects and failed wars in Korea, rebellions broke out.
624 CE: 👤 Born: Empress Wu Zetian — China's only female emperor
670 CE: 📅 War: Tang–Tibet wars — Rise of the Tibetan Empire
751 CE: 📅 War: Battle of Talas — Papermaking spreads west (traditional account)
755 CE: 📅 Uprising: An Lushan rebellion — Millions dead; Tang weakened
794 CE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Heian Japan — 794–1185
850 CE: ⚙ Invention: Gunpowder — China
868 CE: 📜 Manuscript: Diamond Sutra (Dunhuang) — 868 CE
918 CE: 👑 Dynasty: Goryeo — Wang Geon reunified Korea in 918; Goryeo printed the Tripitaka Koreana and the first metal movable type (Jikji, 1377).
960 CE: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Song dynasty — 960–1279
960 CE: 👑 Dynasty: Song dynasty — General Zhao Kuangyin was proclaimed emperor by his troops in 960 and reunified most of China, ending the Five Dynasties chaos.
973 CE: 👤 Born: Murasaki Shikibu — The Tale of Genji

1040: ⚙ Invention: Compass — China
1040: ⚙ Invention: Movable type — China
1132: ⚙ Invention: Cannon — China

1275: 📅 Travel: Marco Polo in China — European fascination with Asia
1368: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Ming dynasty — 1368–1644
1368: 👑 Dynasty: Ming dynasty — Zhu Yuanzhang, a peasant orphan and former monk, led the Red Turban rebellion against the Mongol Yuan and founded the Ming in 1368.
1371: 👤 Born: Zheng He — Treasure voyages
1377: 📜 Manuscript: Jikji — 1377
1392: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Joseon Korea — 1392–1897
1392: 👑 Dynasty: Joseon dynasty — General Yi Seong-gye overthrew the Goryeo dynasty in 1392 and moved the capital to Hanyang (Seoul).
1405: 📅 Exploration: Zheng He's voyages — Ming maritime power
1406: 🗿 Built / founded: Forbidden City — Ming dynasty
1446: 📜 Manuscript: Hunminjeongeum — 1446
Early modern (1500–1800) (7)
1592: 📅 War: Imjin War — Japanese invasion repelled
1600: 📅 War: Battle of Sekigahara — Tokugawa peace
1603: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Edo Japan (Tokugawa) — 1603–1868
1603: 👑 Dynasty: Tokugawa shogunate — Tokugawa Ieyasu won the Battle of Sekigahara (1600) and became shogun in 1603, ending a century of civil war.
1644: 🏛 Civilisation begins: Qing dynasty — 1644–1912
1644: 👑 Dynasty: Qing dynasty — The Manchus, unified by Nurhaci, took Beijing in 1644 after the Ming collapse.
1644: 📅 Conquest: Manchu conquest of China — Qing dynasty
Industrial and imperial age (1800–1945) (8)
1850: 📅 Civil war: Taiping Rebellion — 20–30 million dead
1866: 👤 Born: Sun Yat-sen — Father of the Republic of China
1868: 📅 Politics: Meiji Restoration — Rapid modernisation
1881: 👤 Born: Lu Xun — The True Story of Ah Q
1893: 👤 Born: Mao Zedong — Founded the PRC
1904: 📅 War: Russo-Japanese War — First Asian victory over a European power
1911: 📅 Revolution: Xinhai Revolution — End of imperial China
1927: 📅 Civil war: Chinese Civil War — Communist victory
Contemporary (1945–today) (4)
1945: 📅 War: Atomic bombings of Hiroshima and Nagasaki — ~110,000–210,000 dead
1949: 📅 Revolution: Chinese Revolution — People's Republic founded
1950: 📅 War: Korean War — Divided peninsula
1959: 📅 Famine: Chinese Great Leap Forward famine — Tens of millions died
